01 · Research & Strategy

Fit, sizing, and protective equipment.

Research with the Human Dimensioning Lab at the University of Minnesota. I use 3D scanning, CT imaging, and dimensional analysis to study how protective gear fits the body, with a focus on firefighter gloves.

Published · ITAA Proceedings

Quantifying the dimensional relationship between the interior and exterior of multi-layered protective gloves

Comparing 3D scans of firefighter glove layers showed the largest air gaps and misalignment at the fingertips and the web of the hand. Those are the same areas where dexterity loss matters most on the job.

Approach

How I work in complex systems

A decade running multi-site operations taught me where plans tend to break: at handoffs between teams, tools, and levels of the organization. That is where I focus.

10+ yrsmulti-site operations
80+team members developed
  1. Trace the decision. Follow it from strategy through operations, technology, and the people responsible for it.
  2. Find the handoffs. Locate where intent gets lost between documents, teams, and systems.
  3. Design the workflow. Decide which steps AI handles, which need a person, and how each is checked.
  4. Support adoption. Train, document, and adjust so the new process holds after launch.
