MILO TACHENY

I’m an industrial designer, researcher, and operations leader passionate about creating gear that meets people where they are. I earned my Master of Science in Product Design with a graduate minor in Human Factors and Ergonomics from the University of Minnesota. My work centers on thoughtful design that prioritizes functionality and human-centered innovation.

With over a decade of experience spanning business operations, retail, and healthcare, I bring a unique perspective to the design process while bridging strategy and creativity. Outside the studio, you’ll find me producing media content that highlights the intersection of great design and outdoor exploration, helping people feel seen, equipped, and inspired for their next adventure.
